JPMC Market Expansion and Turkey Project

• Introduction to Turkey Market Expansion  
  JPMC is expanding into a new market by opening a branch in Turkey. This expansion will focus on supporting corporate clients by initiating payments and other services. • Turkey Expansion Timeline and Deliverables  
  The Turkey project is a large-scale initiative with multiple phased releases. The first release is planned for Q1 of next year, focusing on wire transfer payments. The final release is expected by Q4 next year, which will include more complex functionalities such as standing instructions. These features require significant development as they are not currently supported by TSP.

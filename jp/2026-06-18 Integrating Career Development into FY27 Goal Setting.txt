📒Meeting Outline

Understanding FY27 Performance Year and Career Path Integration

• Changes in FY27 Performance Year  
  The FY27 performance year introduces three key changes: employee goals now include career path expectations, career paths are accessible on the hub providing clear level-specific guidance, and Nexus Chat is available to assist employees and managers in creating clear, specific goals. The performance year is structured into four quarters: goal setting with managers in Q1, monthly check-ins in Q2 and Q3 for progress updates and course correction, and year-end performance review in Q4 which impacts ratings, compensation, and promotions.

• Types of Goals and Their Roles  
  Three goal types are emphasized: cascading goals set at leadership level to align the organization, employee goals which are personal and focus on career advancement and skill development specific to practice and level, and people manager goals which are the manager’s accountability for employee development and career conversations.

Career Paths and SMART Goal Setting

• Career Path Overview and Skill Categories  
  Career paths consist of three skill categories: technical skills (e.g., programming languages, certifications), organizational skills (e.g., finance, resource management), and people management skills for those aspiring to lead. Career paths are practical guides accessible on the hub that define expectations at current and next levels.

• Applying SMART Framework to Goals  
  Goals must be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Timely. Example given: instead of vague goals like "Get better at AWS skills," a SMART goal would be "Complete AWS Solution Certification by September and demonstrate application in projects." Specificity in goal setting leads to better accountability and measurable progress.

Employee Responsibilities and Manager Support in Career Development

• Four Key Employee Actions  
  1. Understand and regularly review your career path on the hub.  
  2. Come prepared to goal-setting meetings with clear ideas and skill gap awareness.  
  3. Demonstrate skills actively through assignments, projects, and mentorship.  
  4. Recognize and communicate if you are already performing at the next level to build your promotion case.

• FAQs and Common Concerns  
  Employees are encouraged to proactively discuss career path alignment with managers, set collaborative goals, raise concerns if goals and projects feel disconnected, and understand that promotion depends on demonstrated behaviors and skills, not just goal completion.

Manager’s Role in Goal Setting and Tracking via Workday

• Creating and Assigning Goals  
  Managers can create individual goals for employees via Workday by accessing the employee’s profile and entering goal details. They can also cascade goals to multiple employees or teams using the "Add Goal to Employee" task. Goals can be made editable or locked.

• Monitoring Progress with Milestones  
  Managers can update goals with milestones, including progress notes, due dates, and status updates, to track employee development throughout the year.

• Employee Access to Goals  
  Employees can view their goals in their Workday profile but cannot edit or delete them; only managers have editing rights.

Using Nexus Chat to Enhance Goal Setting and Monitoring

• Goal Improvement and Outcome Focus  
  Nexus Chat helps refine goals by shifting focus from activities to outcomes and impact, providing clearer success criteria.

• Progress Tracking Prompts  
  The platform offers ready-made prompts for monthly and quarterly check-ins, facilitating structured progress tracking and management.

• Role-Specific Goal Generation  
  Users can select practice areas, roles, and career paths to generate detailed job descriptions and enhanced, role-aligned goals, supporting tailored goal setting.

• Best Practices Reminder  
  Users should review and align Nexus Chat suggestions with real-world expectations and business priorities to ensure relevance and effectiveness.

Data Migration & Transformation Issues

  • Use-case level data migration Samir detailed migration work: three initial use cases chosen. Identified issues:

For metrics that are counts, those should be handled as counts rather than mean. Samir will gather detailed examples and follow up with the group. Team to review and adjust queries/transformations or convert data types where required.

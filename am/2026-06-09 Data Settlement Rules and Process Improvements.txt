Data Settlement Rules and Standards

Process and Workflow Adjustments

  Discussed separating tasks and improving data settlement workflow efficiency (data import, updates, and validations).

• Future-Proofing and Scalability:
  Addressed concerns about future conditions not matching current rules. Emphasized removing or adjusting outdated rules to keep the system scalable.

• Coordination and Communication:
  Highlighted the need for clear communication regarding data settlement status, rule updates, and error handling.

📋 Summary of Key Takeaways

• Implement strict data validation/settlement rules (0% null values, specific lengths, valid value sets).
• Identify and resolve duplicate/missing records immediately.
• Design filter criteria carefully to avoid improper data exclusion; remove legacy rules.
• Color-coding (green/yellow) will indicate data quality status.
• Update queries to reflect the latest validation rules and variable descriptions.
• Streamline workflow and communications to support scaling.

Data Quality and Algorithm Selection

• Proposed Method for Threshold Checks  
  Samir presented a proposal to improve the current data quality threshold checking process. Instead of using a single method (fault vendors) based on one metric (e.g., autocorrelation factor, ACF), the proposal involves creating a shortlist of candidate methods (e.g., ARIMA plus, mean standard deviation, EWM) depending on the data type and seasonality. This approach aims to select the best method for threshold evaluation dynamically.

• Discussion on Seasonality and Metric Selection  
  Sachin questioned the rationale behind combining fault vendors and ARIMA plus methods for records with seasonality indicated by ACF values. Bhargav explained that seasonality is identified using the autocorrelation factor and that ARIMA plus is suitable for high seasonality and SKU data. The team agreed to further discuss and clarify the criteria for selecting algorithms based on data characteristics.

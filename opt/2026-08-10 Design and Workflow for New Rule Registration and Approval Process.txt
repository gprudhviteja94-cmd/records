📒Meeting Outline

Design and Workflow of New Rule Registration and Approval Process

• Backend Validation and Form Submission  
  Sachin explained the backend validation process that complements UI validation to prevent invalid or duplicate team registrations. If a user bypasses UI validation, the backend bundle stops the process and throws a business exception, requiring resubmission. This validation bundle was already in place and ensures data integrity before proceeding further.

• Team Registration and Duplicate Checks  
  The system checks if the team is new or existing. For new teams, registration occurs with duplicate name checks to prevent conflicts. Upon successful validation, the rule is saved in the database and displayed in the approval section.

• Rule Execution and Approval Workflow  
  Submitted rules are executed via a daily batch process at midnight to include all submissions up to 12 a.m., avoiding missing late submissions. Execution results are emailed and displayed on the UI. Approval or rejection flags are enabled only after execution results are available. Users cannot approve rules before results are received.

• Reminder and Auto-Rejection Mechanism  
  If no decision is made within three business days, a first reminder is sent; a second reminder follows on the ninth working day. If no action is taken by the 15th day, the rule is auto-rejected, and the business partner is notified via email.

• Approval/Rejection Notifications and Process Termination  
  Upon rejection (manual or auto), the partner receives an email with rejection details. The process stops for rejected rules. Approval triggers the next phase involving the tech team.

• Manual Intervention in Deployment Process  
  Post-approval, a human-in-the-loop downloads the generated SQL queries, logs them into JSON configurations, and manually integrates them into the production environment using the QTrack framework. Full automation is not feasible due to potential query errors, access restrictions, and the need for initial human review before deployment.

• Clarification on Automation Limits and Deployment Steps  
  Sachin clarified that automated deployment is limited to SQL script generation. Code commits, pipeline triggers, and RFC approvals require manual steps and human oversight. The team agreed that skipping approval before deployment is not viable given current constraints.

• Terminology Clarification on Testing  
  The term "testing" was clarified: backend validation of inputs is different from the implementation test run of the entire role. Implementation testing occurs after approval and is distinct from the approval flow.

• Team Involvement and Progress
